About Auriva Ltd

Two continents.
One firm. Zero compromise.

Auriva Ltd was founded in Malta by Simon Vassallo and Kelvin Kiteki — combining Maltese corporate expertise with certified public accounting and financial acumen to serve businesses that operate across borders.

We believe that every business, from a Valletta startup to a Nairobi-linked SME, deserves the same level of financial precision that large firms charge a premium for. That's exactly what we deliver.
